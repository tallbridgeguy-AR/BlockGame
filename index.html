How to draw
  • Press and drag from one dot to another (mouse or finger).
  • Draw every visible edge: the outline of the view, plus a line wherever two surfaces meet at a different depth.
  • Flat surfaces that lie in the same plane have no line between them.
  • Hidden lines are not needed.
  • Stuck? Show Missing marks the lines you left out in dashed orange (no streak credit for that model).
